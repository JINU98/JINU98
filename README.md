## 🧠 About Me

<img src="./jinu.jpg" alt="Profile" width="150" align="right" style="border-radius:10px;"/>

I am a PhD student in Computer Engineering at the **University of South Carolina**, working in the  
**ICAS Lab** with **Prof. Ramtin Zand**.

My research focuses on making **Large Language Models (LLMs)** efficient, scalable, and deployable on
**resource-constrained hardware**.

I work at the intersection of:

- 🔢 **Extreme quantization** (1–2 bit LLMs)
- ⚙️ **Heterogeneous hardware** (Edge TPUs, GPUs, Raspberry Pi, custom accelerators)
